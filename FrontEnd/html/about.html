About us

Making travel planning as exciting as the trip itself.

Our vision

GlobeTrotter aims to become a personalized, intelligent, and collaborative platform that transforms how people plan and experience travel — letting anyone dream, design, and organize a trip with ease in one end-to-end tool that combines flexibility and interactivity.

We picture a world where travelers explore destinations, visualize journeys through structured itineraries, make cost-effective decisions, and share their plans within a community — because planning a trip should feel as good as taking one.

Our mission

We're building a user-centric, responsive application that simplifies the complexity of multi-city travel, giving every traveler intuitive tools to:

  • Add and manage travel stops and durations
  • Explore cities and activities by interest
  • Estimate trip budgets automatically
  • Visualize timelines and plans
  • Share trip plans with others
