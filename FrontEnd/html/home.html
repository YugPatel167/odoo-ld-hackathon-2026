The mission

Built to simplify multi-city planning

Planning a trip across several cities usually means juggling separate tools — one for research, one for budget, one for dates. GlobeTrotter brings it into a single, structured plan: add stops and durations, explore activities by interest, watch your budget update as you go, and see the whole journey as a visual timeline before you ever leave home.
